Casa 02 · Ground floor, carport

Ground floor570 sf
Carport210 sf, not floor area
Home total1,350 sf · 3 bed / 2.5 bath

This floor

  • The front door and the carport door both open into the gallery hall; the hall leads straight to the kitchen, past a built-in bench, coat hooks and a floor-to-ceiling pantry.
  • The stair rises from the kitchen toward the front and lands upstairs behind the cobogó.
  • Carport: open bay under the upper floor, open on the street and outer sides; a 9 ft cedar slat gate, a Lueders limestone pier at the corner and one steel column carry the floor above.
  • Lueders limestone ashlar on the outer wall of the kitchen and living room. Black calçada rug at the carport door and a calçada threshold under the cobogó (about 58 sf, all under the house).

General notes

  • Dimensions to the outside face of exterior walls and the centreline of partitions; areas the same way.
  • Ceilings 9'-6" on the ground floor, 9'-0" upstairs. Doors 8'-0" high; window heads at 8'-6".
  • Stair: 17 risers at 7 7/16 in (10'-6" floor to floor), 16 treads at 10 1/8 in, 3'-3 3/4" clear. Open oak treads with a riser bar (4 in sphere rule); wall handrail at 34–38 in; cedar slats on the open side, 4 in max gaps.
  • Shared wall of a pair: 1-hour fire-resistance-rated, STC 50 or better, continuous to the roof deck, with no openings. Windows go on the front, back and outer side only; vent the dryer and the bath fans through the roof or the outer wall.
  • Visitability (City of Austin, confirm at permit): a no-step entry, ground-floor doors with at least 30 in clear (the WC door 32 in), a ground-floor WC with a 30 × 48 in clear floor space, blocking for grab bars, switches at 48 in max and outlets at 15 in min.
  • Bedrooms have an egress window (5.7 sf clear, sill under 44 in). Smoke and CO alarms in each bedroom, the hall and each floor, interconnected.
  • Mechanical closet under the top of the stair: air handler and a heat-pump water heater; louvred door, and duct the water heater if the closet has under 700 cu ft of air.
