Direction chosen · C, with a cedar gate at the Casita

Casa 02 takes option C as drawn: the two-story cobogó lantern at the entry and stair, cedar gates at the carports. The only change is at the Casita: its cobogó garden wall gets a 4-ft cedar slat gate, the same slats as the Casa 02 carport gates, so the Casita at the street carries both materials. The cedar veil on the back of the upper floor (from option A) is not used. Top: the duo by day and at dusk. Bottom: the alley trio, with the Casita and its gate at the street.
