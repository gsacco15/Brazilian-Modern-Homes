Upper floor, both versions

780 sf · Bed 3 is the den when the suite is downstairs
  • One house, one upper floor, two ground floors. With the carport: 1,350 sf, 3 bed / 2.5 bath. With the bay enclosed as a suite: 1,560 sf, 3 bed + den / 3.5 bath (the upstairs Bed 3 becomes the den).
  • 20 × 39 ft footprint from the test-fit. Mirror it so the bay and the bedrooms are always on the outside wall; stairs, closets and baths then line the shared wall of a side-by-side pair.
  • Facade (direction C): a cobogó screen rises both floors 1 ft in front of the entry and stair, open at the front door; cedar gates close the carport. It lights up at night like Casa 01's screen.
  • The Casa 01 staples, once per home: a Lueders limestone ashlar base on the outer side (a corner pier carries the upper floor), about 50 sf of black calçada at the entries, São Tomé limestone pavers outside.
  • A gallery hall runs from the front door and the carport door to the kitchen. The open-riser oak stair rises from the kitchen behind cedar slats to a landing behind the cobogó: the house's signature.
  • Upstairs side windows are high, or fluted below 5 ft where a bedroom needs an egress window.
  • Ground floor meets Austin's visitability rules: a half bath, and a no-step entry from the carport or the front.
  • Glass to the pátio on the back wall. The shared wall of a pair is a 1-hour fire wall with no openings; on a detached site the suite version takes an 8 ft slider on its outer long wall.
  • Developed plans with dimensions, door and window schedules: Phase2_Casa02_Plans.pdf (sheets A-101 to A-103).

Casita · concept floor plan

Floor plan

640 sf · 1 bed + flex / 1 bath

Notes

  • 640 sf, one story: living and kitchen across the front, a flex room (desk or guest bed) open to living, bedroom, bath, laundry and a walk-in closet at the back.
  • The living room opens to its own pátio through a 10 ft slider on the long wall; mirror the plan to put the pátio on the other side.
  • A covered porch, 7'-9" × 22', runs along the living room and flex room on the pátio side, under a cedar ceiling.
  • At the street its garden wall is cobogó between limestone piers, with a 4 ft cedar slat gate into the pátio.
  • Same frames, stucco, cedar and thin roof edge as Casa 01 and Casa 02.
  • Works with or without its own parking: on a plain 50-ft lot it has none, on corners and wide lots it does.
  • As a backyard home for an existing house it is the Pátio Casita (tracker P2-18).
