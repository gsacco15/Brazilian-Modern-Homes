  • One house, one upper floor, two ground floors. With the carport: 1,350 sf, 3 bed / 2.5 bath. With the bay enclosed as a suite: 1,560 sf, 3 bed + den / 3.5 bath (the upstairs Bed 3 becomes the den).
  • 20 × 39 ft footprint from the test-fit. Mirror it so the bay and the bedrooms are always on the outside wall; stairs, closets and baths then line the shared wall of a side-by-side pair.
  • The open-riser oak stair sits behind cedar slats with a slot window; it is the house's signature.
  • Upstairs side windows are high, or fluted below 5 ft where a bedroom needs an egress window.
  • Ground floor meets Austin's visitability rules: a half bath, and a no-step entry from the carport or the front.
  • Glass to the pátio on the back wall, plus an 8 ft slider on the long wall where the pátio is at the side.

Notes

  • 640 sf, one story: living and kitchen across the front, a flex room (desk or guest bed) open to living, bedroom, bath, laundry and a walk-in closet at the back.
