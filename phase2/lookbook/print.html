The idea

More homes on the same lot, with the same soul

Phase 2 turns Casa 01's language into a repeatable family for Austin's HOME rules.
1

Two or three homes, one lot

Austin's HOME rules allow two or three homes on a standard lot. A pair of Casa 02s makes a duo; add the one-story Casita and it is a trio, on lots 50 to 65 feet wide.

2

One family of parts

Casa 02 and the Casita share Casa 01's palette: white stucco, cedar, cobogó, Lueders limestone and black calçada. Two plans, mirrored and turned, fit every layout.

3

A lantern at the street

A two-story cobogó screen stands over the front doors and the stair. By day it shades and screens; at night the stair light turns it into a lantern.

4

Light from above

Skylights over the stair, the primary shower, the tub, the walk-in and the laundry; a cedar sunshade over the back slider; every home opens to its own pátio.
