Questions

Good to know

Do I need to own land already?

No. Tell us what you're looking for. We can help you check whether a lot fits Casa Pátio before you buy it.

Where do you build?

Austin and the surrounding Hill Country.

Are the prices real quotes?

They're estimates for construction only. Real numbers come from a builder bid on your lot after the Lot Fit Study.

Can I change the design?

Yes. Finishes, bedroom count and size adjust within the Casa Pátio system, and the plan flips or rotates to suit your lot and sun.

What is a cobogó?

A hollow concrete block first made in Recife, Brazil, in 1929. A wall of them shades and ventilates a space while keeping it private.

Are these photos?

They're computer renderings made from the actual plans, materials and Austin sun angles.
