Concept design package / November 2026

Casa 01
Casa Pátio

Brazilian modern for Austin

A 1,670 sf, three-bedroom courtyard house with a covered carport. One thin roof, one private garden, and one limestone wall, built with ordinary Texas framing.

Brazilian soul. Texas materials.
Renders + plans + finishes
01 / The idea

Three moves make the house

Everything else is deliberately ordinary, so the money goes where you can see it.
1

One thin roof

A single 15-inch roof edge runs the full 57-foot street front. Bedrooms, entry canopy and courtyard all sit under one horizontal line.

2

Enter through a garden

A Lueders limestone wall and a full-height cobogó screen hide a private pátio. You open a cedar gate, walk under the canopy, and reach the front door inside the garden.

3

A cool room outside

The great room opens both ways: south to the courtyard, north to a 276 sf veranda under the heritage oak, with the cedar ceiling running straight through.

02 / Street

A long, low line on the street

Limestone wall, cedar pivot gate, cobogó screen and a sliding cedar carport gate, under one 57-foot roof edge. The car is never part of the facade. Front yard: decomposed granite, agave, sotol and Mexican feather grass. No lawn to water.

03 / Dusk

The screen turns into a lantern

Courtyard lights wash the inside face of the cobogó, so the circles glow at night, and the carport lights glow through the gate slats. In-grade grazers rake the limestone. Low path lights mark the stepping slabs.

Courtyard backlight through cobogó2700K in-grade grazers
04 / Arrival

You enter through the garden

On foot through the cedar gate, by car through a cedar side gate from the carport: both land in the covered entry, on one level, at the cedar pivot door.

05 / Pátio

One garden, open to the sky

The pátio wraps behind the carport to the primary bedroom: 778 sf with the covered entry. Dwarf palmetto, ferns and muhly under a limbed-up oak; cedar slats on the carport sides, and a slat screen on the east edge lets the morning light in.

One L-shaped courtyardCedar slat screensLimbed-up oak, native shade garden
06 / Great room

One room between two gardens

Dining and living open south to the pátio and north to the veranda. Cedar ceiling, polished concrete floor, warm-white walls: three materials do most of the work. A low walnut-framed sofa with loose linen cushions faces the room.

Cedar T&G ceilingPolished concrete slabWalnut + linen + leather

Kitchen and gallery

Light oak, black stone and one green tile: a Brazilian palette with materials stocked in Austin.

1Section A-A with Austin solstice sunLooking west
1

Cobogó faces south

It filters low winter sun and summer breezes into the courtyard, and gives privacy from the street without a solid wall.

2

4' eave over south glass

At Austin's latitude (30.3°N) the June noon sun (83°) stops at the courtyard paving. The December sun (36°) reaches 13 feet into the great room.

3

Solid west wall

Bedrooms take side windows only, and the long west wall stays nearly blank against afternoon heat.

4

North veranda

The coolest outdoor room on the lot in July: shaded by the roof and the oak, and open to evening breezes.
